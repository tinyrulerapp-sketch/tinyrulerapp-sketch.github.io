Delete your account Tiny Ruler: Baby Tracker

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open Tiny Ruler and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email tinyruler.app@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We'll delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

DeletedYour account: name, email address, password and app settings.
Deleted if you're the only family memberYour family, your baby's profile and every record (feeds, sleep, diapers, growth, medicine, notes) and invite codes.
Kept if you share the familyThe baby's records stay with the other family members, because they're theirs too. Entries you logged stay, but no longer show your name.
BackupsDeleted information may stay in database backups for up to 30 days, then it's gone for good.
