Privacy Policy Tiny Ruler: Baby Tracker

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • You log your baby's feeds, sleep, diapers, growth and medicine. We store it so it's on your phone and on the phones of family members you invite.
  • Only you and the family members you invite can see your baby's records.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account and your baby's records at any time, right in the app.

Who we are

Tiny Ruler is an app made in South Africa. We are the "responsible party" for your information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You can reach us at tinyruler.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your accountYour name, email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed); we can't read it. We also keep your app language.
Your baby's profileName, birth date, girl or boy, the skin tone you pick for the baby pictures, and settings like the feed and diaper interval.
What you logFeeds (bottle, nursing, solids), sleep, diapers, weight, height and head size, medicines and doses, and notes. Each entry shows which family member logged it.
Your familyWho is in your family and their role, and the invite codes you create to add a partner or caregiver.
On your phone onlyA copy of your records so the app works quickly and offline, your settings, and running timers. Reminders are created on your phone; they don't come from our servers.
Technical logsWhen the app talks to our servers, the servers briefly log technical details like your IP address and the time, for security and to fix problems. These logs are deleted automatically.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. No advertising ID, no tracking, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools. No payment details (the app is free).

How we use it

  • To run the app: save what you log, show it back to you, and keep it in step across your family's phones.
  • To work out countdowns and summaries, like the next feed or today's totals.
  • To send emails you need: a sign-up code or a password-reset code.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see it

Your family. Everyone in your family (people you invited with a code) can see and add to your baby's records. You can remove someone from the family in the app.

Services that help us run the app. They only handle your information to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores your account and records (servers in the European Union, Ireland).
  • Expo delivers app updates to your phone. To do that it sees your IP address and a random install number, but it doesn't receive your records.
  • An email provider sends sign-up and password-reset codes, so it receives your email address.
  • Google Play distributes the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

We will only share information with anyone else if the law requires it.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your information is stored with Supabase in the European Union (Ireland) and travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules in the database make sure each family can only reach its own records. Because our servers are outside South Africa, your information is transferred to the EU, which has data protection laws at least as strong as POPIA.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete it at any time:

  1. Open Tiny Ruler and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This deletes your account straight away. If you were the only member of your family, your baby's profile and all records are deleted too. If you share the family with someone else, the records stay with them and they become the family's owner.

Can't use the app? Email tinyruler.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete your account within 30 days. Deleted information may stay in our database backups for a short time (no more than 30 days) before it's gone for good.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, and you can object to how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself in the app (edit or delete any entry, edit your baby's profile, delete your account). For anything else, email tinyruler.app@gmail.com.

If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). If you live in the EU or UK, you also have rights under the GDPR and can contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Tiny Ruler is for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. The information about babies in the app is entered by their parents or caregivers, who decide what to log and can delete it at any time.

Not medical advice

Tiny Ruler helps you keep track; it doesn't give medical advice. Always talk to a doctor, nurse or clinic about your baby's health.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For big changes we'll also let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tinyruler.app@gmail.com.
